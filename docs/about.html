About

BBX Meta Tracker:

The BBX Meta Tracker is a tool designed to allow anyone to see what parts are getting wins in the current Beyblade X meta. This tracker only lists blades that have appeared in the top 3 decks in a WBO tournament.

About Me:

I'm Christian (they/them), a college student at the Rochester Institute of Technology. I first got into Beyblade during Metal Fight as a kid, and just got back into it during my freshman year in February, 2024. I attended my first tournament around a year later, which was hosted by BBAX. Shortly after I became a member of the Rochester local group, Flower City Bladers.

More recently, I've been involved in the attempted founding of a Beyblade club at RIT, a currently unnofical and unnofiliated group that I serve on the executive board of as Marketing Manager.

I decided to take on this project fairly recently (as of the time of writing this). I realized that since the previous tracker I followed lost support, my view of what is 'meta' has shrunk down to 2 blades (you can probably guess which ones). While I have never done a project like this, I decided to give it a shot!

The main reason I added this tbh was to give the site a personal touch, cause I don't want it to just be a nameless entity run by an AI agent or something.

AI Usage: Near Zero

This site design makes no use of AI. The WBO forum scraper itself is manually run by me, from my laptop, when new entries appear. There aren't any graphics at the time of me writing this, but if I add any, they will be made without AI. My IDE has inline suggestions enabled. This is mostly just a time-saver though. It's autofill can be helpful, but tbh if it tries to do anything more than finish a line, it tends to mess something up so I like to avoid it.
