About

BBX Meta Ranking:

The BBX Meta Ranking is a site made to allow the community to see what parts are getting wins in the current Beyblade X meta. At the moment this tracker only lists blades that have appeared in the top 3 decks in a WBO tournament. But I will be adding a manual submit for BBAX if theres enough interest!

About Me:

I'm Christian (they/them), a college student at the Rochester Institute of Technology. I first got into Beyblade during Metal Fight as a kid, and just got back into it during my freshman year in February, 2024. I attended my first tournament around a year later, which was hosted by BBAX. Shortly after I became a member of the Rochester local group, Flower City Bladers.

More recently, I've been involved in the attempted founding of a college Beyblade club. This is a currently unoffical and unaffiliated group that I serve on the executive board of as Marketing Manager.

I decided to take on this project fairly recently (as of the time of writing this). I realized that since the previous tracker I followed lost support, my view of whats 'meta' has shrunk down to 2 blades (you can probably guess which ones). While I've never done a project like this, I decided to give it a shot!

The main reason I added this was to give the site a personal touch tbh, cause I don't want it to just seem like a faceless entity run by AI or something.

AI Usage: Near Zero

This site design makes no use of AI.

The WBO forum scraper itself is manually run by me, from my laptop when new entries appear.

There aren't any graphics at the time of me writing this, but if I add any, they will be made without AI.

My IDE has inline suggestions enabled. This is mostly just a time-saver though. For auto-complete, I find it can be helpful. Tbh if it tries to do anything more than finish a line, it tends to mess something up so it really has minimal use.
